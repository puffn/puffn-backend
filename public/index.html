Questions & Answers

If you’re unsure about anything, start here. You can always contact us if you need help.

How do I receive my Puffn Steam → Ubi key?
After Stripe confirms your payment, our system automatically assigns an unused key and emails it to the address you entered, along with your payment receipt.
How fast is delivery?
Delivery is instant in most cases. If you don’t see the email within a couple of minutes, check your spam/junk folder. If it’s still missing, contact support and we’ll resend it.
Is my payment safe?
Yes. All payments are processed by Stripe, a global payment provider. We never see or store your card details – we only receive the confirmation that your payment succeeded.
Can I get a refund?
Because keys are digital and one-time use, we cannot refund keys that have already been revealed or redeemed. If your key doesn’t work, contact us and we’ll help you sort it out.
